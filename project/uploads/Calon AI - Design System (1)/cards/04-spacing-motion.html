Body column 688 px / ≈68 ch. Two-column allowed for stat grids and pull-quotes. Page header + footer use mono caption (11px) with 32 px breathing space.

Display type at 96–120 px. Body min 28 px. Always anchor headline to lower-left or upper-left third for editorial weight.

Density · approved use

Calm density

  • → Marketing site, hero, PDF cover. 96 px between sections, 64 px page margin, generous line-height 1.6.
  • → Goal: feel premium, expensive, considered.

Editorial density

  • → PDF body, longform pages, blog. 56 px between sub-sections, 40 px between paragraphs of distinct ideas.
  • → Goal: read like a credible operator's working document.

Operator density

  • → Tables, checklists, stat grids, dashboards. 14–16 px row padding, 13 px caption, mono labels.
  • → Goal: feel like a tool, not a brochure.

→ No pill-shaped buttons. No soft 16+ px radii. Operator-grade UI uses 4–6 px corners. Tags may use full pill (999) at small sizes only.

→ Never glow effects. Where emphasis is needed, use a 1px accent hairline border. Shadows on dark are deeper, blacker — they communicate weight, not light.
