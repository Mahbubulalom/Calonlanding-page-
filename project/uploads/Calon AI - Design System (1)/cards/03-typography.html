During the job

Delivery and margin protection

This is where most service businesses leak the most money — and often don't realise it. Engineers show up without clear scope and spend 30 to 60 minutes clarifying on site.

Time on site isn't tracked, so you don't know if your estimates are accurate. Scope creep happens without approval or charge.

These aren't effort problems. These are system and workflow problems.

Jobs run over without early warning. Scheduling is done ad hoc. Rework happens because original scope wasn't captured.

— £50k to £100k / year on a mid-size service business
