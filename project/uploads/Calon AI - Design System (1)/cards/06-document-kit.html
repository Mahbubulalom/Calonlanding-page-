Revenue leaks before, during, and after the job

Most service businesses don't have a sales problem. They have an operational problem — and it costs money at every stage of the business. Understanding where the leakage happens is the first step to fixing it.

Stage · 01

Before the job

Sales · quoting · response time

Enquiries arrive in multiple places — forms, calls, referrals, emails, WhatsApp, social messages. There's no consistent follow-up system. First response takes 24 to 48 hours instead of 2 to 4 hours.

Creating a quote requires manual data entry from conversations. You don't have a single view of what's been quoted, what's pending, or what's stalled.

Stage · 02

During the job

Delivery · margin protection

This is where most service businesses leak the most money — and often don't realise it. Engineers show up without clear scope and spend 30 to 60 minutes clarifying on site.

Time on site isn't tracked, so you don't know if your estimates are accurate. Scope creep happens without approval or charge. Jobs run over without early warning.

Stage · 03

After the job

Customer lifecycle · repeat work

There's no systematic follow-up after work is complete. Repeat work comes in randomly — if it comes at all. Referrals are sporadic because the relationship isn't maintained beyond the transaction.

These aren't effort problems. These are system and workflow problems. And they cost money at every stage.

CCalon Workflow 03 · Job handover & scope
Workflow · 03

Job handover and scope clarity

When job scope lives in email threads instead of a standard handover document, every job starts with 30 minutes of clarification on site — and ends with rework you absorb.

How it works now
  • ×Scope captured across phone notes, email threads, the founder's memory.
  • ×Engineer arrives on site and spends 30 minutes phoning the office.
  • ×Materials wrong, second visit booked, customer chases for an update.
  • ×Cost absorbed because there's no record of what was originally agreed.
With an operational system
  • →One handover document per job. Standard fields. Customer signs off pre-arrival.
  • →Engineer reads scope on phone in transit. Materials list confirmed.
  • →Job starts within 5 minutes of arrival. No phone calls back to office.
  • →Out-of-scope requests trigger a charge approval, signed by the customer.
